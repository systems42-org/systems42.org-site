title: How to write a post
lede: A draft that shows the post format. Drafts are not published; move the file to _posts/ with a date prefix to publish it.
author: systems42
---

Posts live in `_posts/` and are named `YYYY-MM-DD-slug.md`. The front matter needs a `title`, optionally a `lede` (shown on the blog index and below the headline) and an `author`.

Drafts in `_drafts/` are only rendered with `jekyll serve --drafts`.
